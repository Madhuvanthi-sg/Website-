Who's writing this

I'm in my final year of B.Sc Computer Science, which is the part most people expect. What surprises people is everything that happens outside of it — teaching practice sessions, editing student writing, and, more often than I'd like to admit, singing to myself between assignments.

This notebook is where I keep all of it honest: the code I'm writing, the lessons I'm teaching, and the personal experiences I'd otherwise forget to write down.
